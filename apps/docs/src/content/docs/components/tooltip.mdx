## Name icon-only buttons

Hover a button, or move focus to it with Tab, to show its tooltip. The tooltip repeats the button's aria-label for sighted users; Escape, a click, or moving away hides it. After the first tooltip opens, moving to the next button shows its tooltip at once.

<Example name="tooltip/IconButtons" />

## Placement and shortcuts

placement picks the preferred side; the tooltip flips when there is no room. A Kbd inside the content takes the inverse tooltip colors. Binding the key stays in your app.
