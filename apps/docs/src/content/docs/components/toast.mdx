## Try a toast

Confirm a completed action without interrupting the task. At most three show at once and later ones wait their turn. Toasts dismiss after five seconds; hovering or focusing the region pauses the timer, Escape dismisses the focused toast, and Alt+T moves focus to the region.

<Example name="toast/TryToast" />

## With an action

Offer one short follow-up, such as undo. Choosing the action runs its callback and dismisses the toast.

<Example name="toast/WithAction" />

## Persistent & programmatic

Pass duration: Infinity to keep a toast until it is dismissed. Reusing an id updates the existing toast instead of stacking a new one.
