## Row actions

Open a menu with Enter, Space, the arrow keys, or a click, then move with the arrow keys, Home, and End. Type a letter to jump to a matching item. Escape or Tab closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger.

<Example name="menu/RowActions" />

## Groups, shortcuts, and disabled items

MenuGroup names a set of items with its MenuGroupLabel. Shortcuts are display text; binding the keys stays in your app. Disabled items stay in the list but are skipped by the arrow keys.
