## Paged table

page and onPageChange keep the current page in your state; slicing the rows stays in your app. Previous and Next stay focusable at either end and announce themselves as unavailable.

<Example name="pagination/PagedTable" />

## Many pages

Past seven pages, the first and last pages stay in place and ellipses stand in for the rest. siblingCount sets how many pages show on each side of the current one.
