Accessibility

Interaction notes

  • Use semantic elements first.
  • Keep visible focus states.
  • Provide labels for icon-only controls.

Do

Keep it restrained

Layer near-black surfaces, use muted text hierarchy, and reserve lime for important active states.

Don’t

Avoid visual noise

Don’t use large bright fills, heavy gradients, or saturated colors beyond tiny semantic signals.
